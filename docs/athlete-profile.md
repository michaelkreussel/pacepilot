# Athlete Profile Page

## Route and Ranges

The server-rendered Profile is available at `/profile` and uses `AthleteDataService` as its data
boundary. The route accepts an optional inclusive `end` date and one of these rolling periods:

| Key | UI label | Days | Training buckets |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `day` | Tag | 1 | Daily |
| `week` | 7 Tage | 7 | Daily |
| `month` | 28 Tage | 28 | Seven-day, clipped to the range |
| `3m` | 12 Wochen | 84 | Seven-day, clipped to the range |
| `year` | Jahr | 365 | Seven-day, with a final partial bucket |

Health charts preserve every requested calendar date and serialize missing measurements as null.
Training timelines query the exact selected range. Rolling 28-day values may use the preceding 27
days, but selected-period bars never include activities outside the selected range.

## Page Structure

1. A PacePilot Readiness hero shows score, German label, confidence, formula version, and component
   bars. It explicitly says that the value is not Garmin Training Readiness.
2. Current-state cards show only available metrics and retain their source dates.
3. Health and recovery charts show available personal trends and baselines.
4. Training cards summarize the selected period without combining incompatible sport volume.
5. Training charts show exact-range volume, frequency, intensity, Training Effect, and Garmin load
   only where source metrics exist.
6. Per-sport volume remains separated by Garmin activity type.
7. Six recent workouts provide bounded drill-down into the existing activity analysis pages.

The page uses the existing PacePilot shell, colors, typography, card language, Chart.js dependency,
and responsive breakpoints. A dedicated `profile.css` handles the readiness composition and dense
chart grids. No frontend framework or build step was added.

## Metrics and Charts

Current cards can include:

- resting HR, HRV, sleep duration, Body Battery, and stress;
- Garmin Training Readiness and recovery time;
- VO2max, Garmin Training Status, and Garmin acute/current load.

Health charts are omitted individually when unavailable:

- resting HR with personal baseline;
- HRV with personal baseline;
- sleep duration with Garmin sleep need;
- Garmin sleep score;
- stress and Body Battery high;
- VO2max;
- Garmin Training Readiness.

Training charts are also availability-driven:

- running distance with rolling 28-day running volume;
- total training duration;
- workout frequency;
- aerobic and anaerobic Training Effect;
- per-activity Garmin Exercise Load;
- Garmin training/acute/chronic load history;
- one explicitly labelled sport and HR/power zone distribution;
- moderate versus vigorous intensity minutes.

Garmin Exercise Load and Garmin training load remain distinct. Neither is replaced by distance,
duration, Training Effect, or a fabricated value when unavailable.

## Drill-Down

Recent workout cards link to `/activities/<activity id>`. Clickable training buckets link to the
activity index with inclusive `from` and `to` filters. The activity route performs the date filtering
in SQLite and remains user-scoped. Health points link to the selected day on the Profile.

Chart JavaScript only draws render-ready labels, values, null gaps, and links generated by the server.
It does not aggregate Garmin data or infer metric meaning.

## Missing and Partial Data

- Missing health and Garmin fitness values are never displayed as zero.
- Unsupported Garmin metrics do not create empty cards or charts.
- An unsynchronized activity history shows a sync action instead of confirmed zero workouts.
- A synchronized empty period can report zero workouts without implying missing history.
- Short histories render isolated points and expose PacePilot readiness confidence.
- One-year ranges are bounded to 365 health points and at most 53 training buckets.
- Old activities with absent duration, distance, load, zones, or Training Effect remain valid and do
  not suppress richer recent activities.

## Remaining Improvements

- Add a deterministic sleep-night DTO before implementing a stage hypnogram and sleep-score component
  breakdown. Stored stage timestamps are naive Garmin GMT values, so local clock presentation first
  needs explicit timezone semantics.
- Add activity-index sport and zone controls to complement the implemented date drill-down.
- Add editable athlete goals, thresholds, and profile attributes when those concepts exist in the
  domain model.
- Consider server-side weekly health downsampling if ranges longer than one year are introduced.
